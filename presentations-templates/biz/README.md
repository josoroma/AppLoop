# Biz — Corporate Technology Aesthetic Presentation Template

Corporate technology aesthetic presentation template using a dark mode with a global black gradient background per slide, rounded cards, thin borders, restrained shadows, clean typography, generous spacing, white headings, muted gray body text, and cyan-to-blue gradient accents; preserving each visual's intended composition and aspect ratio.

## Slides Overview

1. **Cover** — Minimal cover with small abstract logo top-left, large lower-left title "Business Innovation." with "Innovation." in gradient, three-line placeholder copy top-right, geometric squares, copyright bottom-right.
2. **Driving Innovation** — "Driving Innovation for Business Growth." with "Business Growth." in gradient, two balanced subtitle-and-body columns, partial gradient ring bottom-left, translucent square top-right.
3. **About Us** — Two-column layout with introductory and secondary paragraphs plus a gradient website button on the left, and a large text in a circle with border gray on the right.
4. **Financial & Growth Projections** — Asymmetric layout with supporting text on the left, a circle with text on the right, plus a bottom metric card showing "430K", "Definition of Innovation", and one explanatory line.
5. **Our Innovative Approach** — Centered title above four equal vertical cards with simple chart, shield/finance, balance/strategy, and database icons, each containing "Subtitle Here" and short body text, with the second card active in a cyan-to-blue gradient and the others dark gray.
6. **The People Making It Happen** — Centered title with "People" in blue, three equal team cards with circle avatars, gradient role badges, and names "Jonathan Doe" — "CEO Protagonist", "Angelica Doe" — "CFO Protagonist", "Abraham Doe" — "Marketing".
